# Privacy Policy — BubbleQuest: Color Blast

Effective date: November 7, 2026
Developer: Mahananda Reddy Yedamakanti
Contact: whyzomd@gmail.com
App: BubbleQuest: Color Blast (package org.ykmahanand.bubblequest), Android

This policy explains what information the game does and does not handle.

## The game itself collects nothing

BubbleQuest: Color Blast has no user accounts, no sign-up, no login, and no
server operated by the developer. The game does not ask for your name, email,
phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location.

Your game progress, scores, settings and in-game wallet are stored only on
your own device, in the game's local save file. That information never leaves
your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To load and measure ads, the
Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (consent) may
process:

- IP address (used for approximate, coarse location)
- Device information (model, operating system, language)
- Ad interaction and impression data (which ads were shown or tapped)
- Diagnostics and performance data for the ads SDK
- A device advertising identifier, where the operating system and your
  choices allow it

This processing is done by Google as described in Google's own policies:
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Where required, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent
message before requesting ads, and provides a privacy options entry in the
game's settings so you can change that choice. Ads are configured with a
maximum content rating of G.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. It is a general-audience
puzzle game and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone,
including children.

## Sharing

The developer does not sell personal data and does not share data with
anyone other than the Google advertising services described above, which the
game needs in order to show ads.

## Your choices

- You can limit ad personalisation in your device settings (Google settings
  > Ads) and reset your advertising identifier there at any time.
- You can change your ads consent choice in the game's Privacy settings.
- You can delete all locally stored game data by clearing the app's data or
  uninstalling the game.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same
address with a new effective date.
