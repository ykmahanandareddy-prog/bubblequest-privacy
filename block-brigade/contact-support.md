# Block Brigade — Contact Support

Developer: Mahananda Reddy Yedamakanti (MD).

Email [whyzomd@gmail.com](mailto:whyzomd@gmail.com) with the subject “Block Brigade — Support”. Include your device model, Android version, game version, level number and a brief description. Screenshots are helpful. Do not send passwords, payment details, or unnecessary personal information.
