# Block Brigade — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026. Developer: Mahananda Reddy Yedamakanti (MD). Contact: whyzomd@gmail.com.

Block Brigade is a tower rescue puzzle game. No player account or login is required. Progress, settings, privacy acknowledgement and reminder choices are stored on your device. The developer does not operate a gameplay account server. Device backup behavior is controlled by Android and your device settings; clearing app data or uninstalling may remove local progress.

## Advertising

The game integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and User Messaging Platform. Google may process IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers where permitted, device information, ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver and measure advertisements. Development/debug builds use Google sample advertising IDs. Release builds, including Play testing releases, use the configured publisher advertising IDs. See [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google partner services information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Where required and available, Google's consent form provides advertising choices. A privacy options control appears in Settings when Google requires it. Acknowledging this policy does not grant advertising consent or notification permission.

## Optional local reminders

The first launch presents a separate notification choice after privacy acknowledgement. Reminders stay off until you choose Enable and grant any required Android permission. Choosing Not now or denying permission ends the automatic offer; the app does not repeatedly prompt. You can change reminders in game Settings and notification permission in Android settings. Android 13 and later control notification permission with a system dialog; earlier versions may not display that dialog.

Reminders are scheduled locally after 10 hours away, respect quiet hours from 22:00 to 09:00, and occur at most once per 24 hours. Timing may vary with Android scheduling and battery restrictions. Notification choices are independent of privacy acknowledgement and advertising choices.

## Other choices and support

The game does not request contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location. Sharing opens Android's share interface only when you select Share. Public policy/support buttons open an external browser; that provider handles its own connection information. If you email support, your message and voluntarily supplied information are processed to answer your request. Do not include passwords or unnecessary personal details.

The game is a general-audience puzzle and is not directed at children under 13. Contact whyzomd@gmail.com about privacy or deletion of information you provided to support. You can remove local game data through Android settings. Policy updates will be published here with a revised date.
