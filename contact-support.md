## 2. Report a Technical Problem

If you experience a problem, please email us with the following information:

**Email Subject:** BubbleQuest — Technical Support

Please include:

1. Your device model (for example, Samsung Galaxy A22).
2. Your Android version.
3. Your BubbleQuest application version.
4. The level number where the problem occurred.
5. A brief description of the issue.
6. A screenshot, if available.

Please do not include passwords, payment details, or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions

### Is BubbleQuest free to play?

Yes. BubbleQuest is designed as a free-to-play Android game supported by advertisements.

### Does BubbleQuest require an account?

No. BubbleQuest does not currently require an online account to play.

### Can I unlock cannons without paying?

Yes. Cannons are designed to be unlockable through gameplay progress or earned virtual coins.

### Does BubbleQuest require an internet connection?

Core gameplay is designed to work offline. Internet connectivity may be required to load advertisements or access external support resources.

### Where is my game progress stored?

Game progress is designed to be stored locally on your device.

Uninstalling the application or clearing its data may remove saved progress, depending on your device's backup settings.
