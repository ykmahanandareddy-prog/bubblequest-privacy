## Game and technical support

For aiming, hands/gun animation, bird routes, tunnels, level objectives, stars, saved progress, landscape layout, sound or performance issues, email **whyzomd@gmail.com** with the subject **Tunnel Wings — Support**.

Include the game version, device model, Android version, level number, what happened and how to reproduce it. A screenshot is optional. Do not send passwords, payment details, advertising identifiers or unnecessary personal information.

## Advertising and extra shots

The current Android test build uses Google's sample ads, which should be marked **Test Ad**. Do not tap an ad's install or content area merely to test it. Use the ad's normal completion/close controls.

A rewarded ad after a failed attempt can add four shots once while keeping that attempt's progress. Another rewarded retry starts the level with normal ammunition. A newly completed level counts toward the completion ad that appears after two new completions. Rewarded ads do not reset that counter; restarting the app does. See the [Terms of Use](tunnel-wings-terms-of-use.md) for the full rules.

If an ad fails to load, wait for connectivity and availability to recover. Closing an ad before the SDK confirms and completes the reward does not grant extra shots. If the game offers **Retry Saving**, use it while that session is available. For an unexpected result, report the approximate time, level, game version and whether the ad was completed and closed. Do not include private information visible in another app.

## Privacy and deletion

For privacy requests, use the subject **Tunnel Wings — Privacy Request**. The [Privacy Policy](tunnel-wings-privacy-policy.md) explains Google advertising-service processing and support correspondence.

**Settings → Privacy Choices** appears when Google's consent SDK requires that control. Android/Google advertising settings may offer additional controls.

There is no game account or developer cloud save. To delete local game data, use Android **Settings → Apps → Tunnel Wings → Storage → Clear data**, or uninstall the app. This removes local progress and preferences and cannot be reversed through a developer account. It does not delete information already held independently by Google. You may email us to request deletion of support correspondence or other information controlled by the developer, subject to applicable obligations.
