# Buffalo Run — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

Buffalo Run is developed by Mahananda Reddy ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles and how it is used.

## Information the game handles

- **Game progress on your device.** Buffalo Run stores your game progress, settings, earned coins, and owned items locally on your device. This data stays on your device unless you use a backup or transfer feature provided by your device or Google Play.
- **Purchases.** In-app purchases (the Herd Pass subscription, boost packs, and cosmetic items) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your card or bank details. Google Play provides us with purchase confirmation information (such as the product purchased and order status) so the game can deliver what you bought.
- **Advertising.** Buffalo Run shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers and usage information to serve and measure ads, subject to your consent choices. Where required (for example in the EEA/UK and certain US states), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent tools before personalized ads are served. You can change your choices in the game's privacy settings where available.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell your personal information.
- We do not require an account to play.
- We do not collect your name, email address, or contacts through the game. If you email us for support, we use your message only to respond to you.

## Children

Buffalo Run is intended for teens and adults (ages 13 and above). It is not directed at children under 13.

## Your choices

- You can manage or cancel the Herd Pass subscription at any time in Google Play (Play Store → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions).
- You can reset your ad consent choices in the game's privacy settings where available, and control ad personalization in your device settings.
- Uninstalling the game removes locally stored game data from your device.
