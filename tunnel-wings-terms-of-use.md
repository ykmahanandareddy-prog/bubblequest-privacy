## Advertisements and the four-shot bonus

The current Android test version uses Google's sample advertisements. Production advertising is not enabled. Ads require connectivity, SDK availability and appropriate privacy permission; their availability or completion time is not guaranteed.

The game's current rules are:

1. Every two newly completed levels in the current app session trigger a completion-ad requirement. Replaying an already completed level does not add to this counter.
2. After a failure, a rewarded ad can add **four shots** to the same attempt, keeping its hits and score. Unhit birds return so the shots can be used. This bonus is available once per attempt and does not guarantee a win.
3. If the attempt fails again, a rewarded retry restarts that level with its normal ammunition and a fresh attempt score. Watching an ad does not itself unlock the next level.
4. A rewarded ad must report an earned reward and close successfully before the reward is applied. Closing early, a failed presentation or an interrupted app session may leave no applied reward. Duplicate callbacks do not grant duplicate shots.
5. Winning with bonus shots counts as a normal new completion. Watching a rewarded ad does **not** reset the completion-ad counter. Consequently, a rewarded ad and a completion ad can occur close together when that win is the second new completion.
6. Restarting the app, including after restarting the phone, resets the completion-ad counter to zero and clears previous pending ad requirements. Earned stars and level progress remain saved. The unfinished attempt restarts normally; bonus ammunition and its running bird simulation are not checkpointed across app closure.

After an ad reward is earned, a temporary save error can be retried without another ad while the same session and earned credit remain available. The game does not promise recovery of an unfinished ad or in-memory choice after process termination. Rewards have no monetary value and are not sold or transferable.
