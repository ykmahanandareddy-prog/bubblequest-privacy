## Game and technical support

For crashes, saved progress, character movement, block colors, five return slots, sound, reminders or advertising problems, email us with the subject **Block Orbit — Support**.

Include your device model, Android version, game version, level number and a description of what happened. You may attach a screenshot. Do not send passwords, payment information or unnecessary personal information.

## Advertising and rewarded continues

If an ad fails, closes unexpectedly or a completed rewarded ad does not free a slot, describe what happened and the approximate time. Advertising availability depends on connectivity, inventory and consent. Do not tap an advertisement just to test it.

## Privacy requests

For questions about advertising consent or personal information, email **whyzomd@gmail.com** with the subject **Block Orbit — Privacy Request**. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion of information controlled by the developer.

See our [Privacy Policy](block-orbit-privacy-policy.md) and [Terms of Use](block-orbit-terms-of-use.md). In the game, go to **Settings → Reminders & privacy → Privacy & choices**. Manage privacy choices appears when Google's consent SDK requires it.

## Local progress and reminders

Game progress and settings are saved on your device. There is no game account or cloud backup. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app deletes that local progress.

Reminders are optional local notifications. Turn them off in the game's reminder settings or Android notification settings.

## Feedback

Suggestions about puzzles, graphics, accessibility and performance are welcome at **whyzomd@gmail.com**.
