## 3. Game Description

Bubble Shooter is a bubble-matching puzzle. Players aim a toy cannon, bounce shots from walls and connect three or more bubbles of one color. Disconnected groups fall. Clearing the board unlocks the next level. Running out of shots or reaching the danger line ends the attempt. Only the current ten-level chapter is displayed; completing it reveals the next ten.

Features may change through updates.
