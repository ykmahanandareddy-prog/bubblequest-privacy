## 3. Game Description

Block Orbit is a color-matching block puzzle. Players choose characters carrying toy blasters that travel around a board and clear exposed blocks of their own color. Characters with ammunition return to five parking slots. Filling all five slots ends the attempt. Clearing the board advances automatically to the next generated level.

Features may be added, modified, or discontinued through future updates.
