# Privacy Policy — Tunnel Wings

**Effective date:** November 14, 2026  
**Application:** Tunnel Wings (`org.tunnelwings.game`), Android  
**Developer/Publisher:** Mahananda Reddy Yedamakanti · MD (Independent Developer)  
**Privacy and support contact:** [whyzomd@gmail.com](mailto:whyzomd@gmail.com)

This policy describes information handled by Tunnel Wings and its advertising services. The game has no account registration, developer-operated game server, cloud synchronization, in-app purchases or subscriptions.

## Game information stored on your device

Level progress, best stars and settings such as music, sound, vibration and aiming sensitivity are saved locally in the app's storage. The developer's game code does not upload those saves to a server. Advertising counters and pending choices are local game state; starting a new app session clears the session's ad requirements without erasing earned stars.

The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, precise location or background location. It does not contain a separate developer analytics or crash-upload service, notification service, or social login. Advertising services can still process the information described below.

Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes its local game data. There is no game account or developer cloud backup from which we can restore that progress. Clearing local storage does not delete information already held independently by Google or your device/platform provider.

## Google advertising and consent services

The Android test version uses Google's sample ad identifiers with the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Test ads do not earn advertising revenue, but loading them still contacts Google; test mode does not mean that no data is processed. Production ad serving is currently disabled. This policy will be reviewed before a production advertising release.

Depending on the SDK configuration, permissions, region and your choices, Google advertising services may collect or share:

- IP address, which can indicate approximate location; no location permission is requested by the game.
- Device, operating system, language and app information.
- App/ad interactions, including launches, impressions, taps and video views.
- Diagnostics and performance information related to the app and advertising SDK.
- Advertising, app-set, device or account-related identifiers where available and permitted by the platform and configuration.

Google uses advertising-service data for purposes such as delivering and measuring ads, analytics, security and fraud prevention. The test APK removes the Android advertising-ID permission, but that does not prevent IP-based processing or necessarily prevent other permitted SDK identifiers. Publisher first-party ID is disabled, and ad requests have a maximum content rating of G. An ad content rating is not a guarantee that an app is suitable for every age.

We request updated consent information through UMP at startup and gate ad requests on the SDK's permission to request ads. Where Google requires a privacy-options entry, **Settings → Privacy Choices** opens Google's controls. That entry may be absent when the SDK says it is not required. A decision to view this policy or the game's Terms is separate from advertising consent.

Google explains its processing and retention in its own policies:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from apps and sites using its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Mobile Ads data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/next-gen/privacy/play-data-disclosure)

The game shows completion ads at eligible level boundaries and offers rewarded ads after a failed attempt. The [Terms of Use](tunnel-wings-terms-of-use.md) explain the four-shot reward and restart behavior. An earned ad reward never grants Google access to the contents of the game's local save through a developer-operated server.

## Support messages and external pages

If you choose to email support, we receive the email address, message and attachments you send. We use them to answer your request and handle related technical, security or legal issues. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information. Support correspondence is retained as needed for those purposes; you can request deletion of information under the developer's control, subject to applicable obligations.

Opening a legal or support link launches an external browser. Public legal pages are hosted by GitHub, which handles visits under its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Your email provider, browser and any external service you choose to visit have their own policies. The developer does not automatically send a support email when you open the Support page.

Following the publisher's general-audience policy, Tunnel Wings is intended for people aged 13 and older, subject to any higher applicable local age requirement. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not ask for a date of birth or create child profiles. Parents or guardians with concerns about information sent to support can contact the address above. Store audience and advertising configuration must match the actual release; this statement is not a substitute for those settings.

## Sharing, retention and choices

The developer does not sell your local game saves or operate a service that receives them. Google processes advertising-service information as described above; we do not control Google's independent retention periods. Local saves remain until removed by you, the app, or device storage management. Support correspondence is handled as described above.

You can:

- Use the game's Privacy Choices entry when Google requires it.
- Manage advertising controls available in Android/Google settings; available controls vary by device and OS version.
- Delete local progress through Android **Settings → Apps → Tunnel Wings → Storage → Clear data**, or uninstall the app. This is destructive to local progress.
- Ask about access, correction, deletion or other rights concerning information controlled by the developer by emailing **whyzomd@gmail.com**. Applicable rights depend on your location. Requests concerning Google's independently held information should also use Google's privacy controls.
